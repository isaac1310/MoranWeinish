About me

I care about the whole journey - not just the pretty screens.

I’m a Product & UX/UI Designer who enjoys getting to the heart of a problem, understanding what people actually need, and turning complexity into clear, thoughtful experiences. I work across research, flows, systems and high-fidelity UI, always looking at how the pieces come together to create a product that feels simple, purposeful and easy to use.

Good design isn’t just about how a product looks. It’s about making everything click.
