Refreshing Suzuki’s Digital
Experience

A visual refresh of Suzuki Israel’s website, designed to bring the brand’s digital presence up to date while staying true to its familiar identity. The new UI creates a cleaner, more consistent experience across pages, components, and screen sizes, with a stronger focus on clarity, usability, and the cars themselves.

01 / Overview

Bringing Suzuki’s Brand Into the Digital Experience

As part of the Comrax team, I led the visual refresh of Suzuki Israel’s website, with the goal of creating a more modern, clear, and consistent digital experience while keeping the brand familiar and recognizable.

The project covered the visual language across the full website. From the homepage and model pages to trade-in, services, forms, content, and mobile. The challenge was to bring all these different areas together under one design approach, while giving the vehicles a stronger presence and making information and key actions easier to find.

The new UI builds on Suzuki’s practical and approachable character through clear hierarchy, strong imagery, focused use of the brand colors, and a consistent set of components that works across pages and screen sizes.

02 / Why Refresh?

Evolving the Experience, Not Replacing It

Suzuki Israel already had a familiar digital presence and a clear brand identity. The goal wasn’t to redesign everything from scratch, but to improve how that identity works across a large and content-rich website.

The refresh focused on clearer hierarchy, stronger consistency, better use of space, and a more modern visual language, while keeping the experience familiar for existing users.

03 / Approach

What Guided the Design

The visual refresh was built around a set of clear design principles: keeping Suzuki’s identity recognizable while creating a cleaner, more consistent, and easier-to-use experience across the site.

  • Brand-Led Visual Language

    Suzuki’s red, logo, typography, imagery, and graphic elements were brought together into a clear and consistent digital language, with the vehicles always at the center.

  • Clear Visual Hierarchy

    Page layouts were designed to make content easier to scan, with clear sections, strong headings, and visible actions that help users understand where to go next.

  • Responsive by Design

    Layouts and components adapt across desktop, tablet, and mobile, with spacing, navigation, and interactions adjusted for smaller screens and touch.

  • Accessibility

    Contrast, text sizes, interaction states, and touch targets were considered throughout the UI to support an accessible and comfortable browsing experience.

  • Interactive Components

    Complex information such as model comparisons, price lists, specifications, and emission ratings was designed to be easier to explore and understand.

  • Responsive Media

    Video and visual content were integrated into the page layouts without interrupting the browsing experience, adapting naturally across screen sizes.

04 / Design

Bringing the New Visual Language to Life

Suzuki’s red is used with purpose and highlighting key actions and important elements without taking over the interface. The layouts combine strong vehicle imagery, clear typography, and generous spacing to create a clean, recognizable Suzuki experience across the site.

Homepage / Brand Experience A full width driving video creates an immediate sense of motion and brand experience, supported by a clear primary action that guides users to the next step. Model Range / Cars First A product first layout puts each car at the center, with key specifications: engine, safety and emissions. Grouped into one clear, scannable strip for easier comparison. Suzuki Story / Social Content Social content is brought into the website through a consistent feed, connecting the Suzuki community and lifestyle with the digital brand experience. Model Page / “What’s Your Color?” Interactive configurator moments turn exploration into a more personal experience, allowing users to discover the model through colors, details and strong visual storytelling. Model Page / Visual Gallery An editorial image grid replaces the traditional gallery approach, combining exterior, interior and lifestyle photography to create a richer and more dynamic product page. Suzuki Trade In / Advanced Search A streamlined used car search brings the key filters upfront, helping users quickly narrow hundreds of available vehicles by model, year, ownership, mileage and location. Trade-In / Vehicle Results A clean, consistent vehicle grid makes a large inventory easy to scan, surfacing the information that matters most: price, model, year, mileage and ownership before entering the vehicle details. Trade-In / Vehicle Quick View Vehicle details and lead generation are brought together in a focused modal, allowing users to explore a car and take action without losing their place in the search journey. Service & Contact / One Service Hub High frequency customer actions are consolidated into one clear service hub, from finding a nearby showroom or service center to leaving details and accessing essential self-service actions.

05 / The Transformation

Seeing the Refresh Side by Side

Looking at the old and refreshed experiences side by side shows how small, focused design decisions changed the overall feel of the site. Stronger hierarchy, more space, clearer actions, and a more consistent use of Suzuki’s visual language created a cleaner experience without losing the familiarity of the original.

06 / Design system

A Consistent System for Suzuki

The design system brings Suzuki’s digital language into one consistent set of colors, typography, icons, buttons, cards, and UI components. Suzuki red is used as the main action color, supported by a neutral palette, a clear type scale, and a custom icon set designed for vehicle specs and key information.
